How it works

1. Mint up to 5 FireUn (0.0001 ETH each). 88.88% of the fee goes to the deployer, 11.12% to the prize pool.
2. Each round's 888 winning token IDs are committed by hash on-chain before the reveal — the draw can't be rigged after the fact.
3. After the reveal, winners claim their tier share and every other ticket claims its dividend. Both pay into the ticket's own vault (its ERC-6551 token-bound account); the ticket owner withdraws from there.
4. Claim window is 90 days per round. Unclaimed prizes stay in the pool and fund the next round — nothing is ever lost.
5. Rounds repeat forever, funded by the 5% resale royalties — split 50/50 between the deployer and the prize pool.
6. Art rarity is cosmetic only: every ticket has identical lottery odds, whatever its portrait looks like.
